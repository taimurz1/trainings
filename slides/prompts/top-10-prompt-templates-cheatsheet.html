Top 10 Prompt Templates Cheatsheet

Essential structural frameworks for foundational prompt engineering.

1. The Role-Task-Format (RTF) Pattern Foundational

Establishes identity, goal, and structure in a single clear directive.

Act as a [ROLE]. Your task is to [TASK]. Provide the response in [FORMAT, e.g., Markdown table/Bullet points] using a [TONE] tone.
2. Few-Shot Exemplar Prompt Formatting

Demonstrates expected output styling through explicit input-output pairs.

Format the provided input to match the example outputs below.

Example 1:
Input: High priority bug in login API
Output: [SEVERITY: High] | [MODULE: Auth] | [SUMMARY: Login API failure]

Input: [INSERT YOUR TEXT HERE]
Output:
3. Constraint & Negative Prompting Control

Sets explicit guardrails to remove unwanted fluff, jargon, or long-winded answers.

Explain [TOPIC] for [TARGET AUDIENCE].
Constraints:
- Maximum word count: [WORD COUNT]
- Do NOT use filler buzzwords like "game-changer", "delve", or "seamless".
- Output MUST be structured in bullet points only.
4. Chain-of-Thought (Step-by-Step) Prompt Reasoning

Forces the AI to show its logical reasoning before declaring a final answer.

Solve the following problem: [INSERT PROBLEM]
Please break down your solution step-by-step. Show your reasoning for each step before presenting the final summary.
5. Audience Persona Adjuster Tone

Adapts complex explanations for distinct technical levels or user roles.

Explain [CONCEPT] three separate times:
1. To a 10-year-old using a real-world analogy.
2. To a junior non-technical project manager.
3. To an enterprise executive focusing purely on business value.
6. Socratic Mentor Prompt Learning

Instructs the AI to guide you with questions rather than just feeding you answers.

Act as a patient mentor in [SUBJECT]. Do not give me the direct answer. Instead, ask me targeted questions one at a time to lead me to the solution myself.
7. Perspective Switching (Red Teaming) Analysis

Helps evaluate ideas by generating counterarguments and alternate views.

Review the following proposal: "[INSERT PROPOSAL]"
Provide feedback from two viewpoints:
1. An advocate pointing out top strengths.
2. A skeptical critic identifying key risks and potential failure points.
8. Output Delimiter Tagging Structure

Isolates context or input text clean from instructions using custom tags.

Analyze the text enclosed in  tags below and summarize 3 actionable points.


[PASTE TEXT HERE]
9. Self-Correction / Quality Audit Refinement

Asks the AI to review and upgrade its own draft output before final delivery.

Review your previous response to check for inaccuracies, vagueness, or formatting errors. Rewrite the response to make it crisper, more actionable, and 20% shorter.
10. Universal Meta-Prompt Template Creator

Uses the AI to build optimized prompts for you for future tasks.

I want to accomplish [GOAL]. Ask me 3 clarifying questions you need answered to build the optimal prompt template for this task.
