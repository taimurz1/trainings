What is Prompt Engineering?

Mental Model
🍕 Ordering Food (Vague)

If you tell a waiter "Give me food," you might get a soup you don't like.

AI Parallel: Asking "Write an essay" gives a generic, boring response.

🍕 Ordering Food (Specific)

If you order "A medium thin-crust chicken pizza with extra cheese," you get exactly what you want!

AI Parallel: Giving Context + Role + Task + Format gives a perfect answer.

3 Core Concepts You Need Today

Glossary
1. Prompt: The exact instruction or question typed into the AI chat box.
2. Role: The persona or job assigned to the AI (e.g., "Act as a High School Biology Teacher").
3. Output: The final answer, table, or text generated by the AI model.
Prompt Engineering 101 Slide 4 of 10

The "C-R-T-O" Prompting Formula

Core Framework
1. C
Context

Who are you & what is the background?

2. R
Role

Who should the AI pretend to be?

3. T
Task

What specific action to take?

4. O
Output

What format (table, list, essay)?

Formula: [Context] + [Role] + [Task] + [Output Format]

Step 1: Setting Context & Role

Direct Comparison
❌ WEAK WAY ✅ RIGHT WAY
"Explain photosynthesis." "I am a 10th-grade student preparing for exams. Act as a high school biology teacher and explain photosynthesis..."
⚠️ Watch Out: Never leave the AI guessing who the answer is for!
Prompt Engineering 101 Slide 6 of 10

Step 2: Giving Task & Output Format

Direct Comparison
❌ WEAK WAY ✅ RIGHT WAY
"Tell me about Pakistan's history." "List 5 key events in Pakistan's history between 1947 and 1970. Present the answer as a 2-column table with 'Year' and 'Event'."
🎯 Definition of Success: Specifying a table format makes the output clean, scannable, and ready to use!
Prompt Engineering 101 Slide 7 of 10

Step 3: Refining Bad Answers

Course Correction
📏 Too Long?

Type: "Shorten this to 3 simple bullet points under 50 words."

🧩 Too Hard?

Type: "Explain this like I am 12 years old using a food analogy."

🔍 Missing Detail?

Type: "Add 2 real-world industry examples to point #2."
