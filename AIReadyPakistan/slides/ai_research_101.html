The Golden Rule of AI Research

Academic Integrity
❌ WRONG WAY (Unethical)

Copying text directly from ChatGPT into your assignment without checking facts or citations.

✅ RIGHT WAY (Ethical)

Using AI to summarize complex papers, then rewriting key findings in your own words with verified links.

3 Vocabulary Words You Need Today

Glossary
1. Literature Review: Reading and summarizing existing research articles on a specific topic.
2. Citation: Giving proper credit to the original author or scientist who published the research.
3. Hallucination: When basic AI makes up fake facts or non-existent citations (we will teach you how to avoid this!).

Step 2: Summarizing Difficult Papers

Extraction Prompt
"Summarize this PDF paper in 3 structured parts: 1) What core problem were the researchers solving? 2) What methodology or data did they use? 3) What were their top 2 conclusions?"
⚠️ Watch Out: Always link or upload the exact PDF to stop AI models from making up imaginary statistics!

Step 3: Verification Checklist

Quality Control
1. Click the Citation Link: Check if the research paper actually exists online.
2. Check Author & Year: Make sure the paper date matches the context of your study.
3. Write in Your Own Voice: Never copy-paste AI responses directly into assignments.
